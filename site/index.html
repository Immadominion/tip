tip

Money that is nobody else’s business.

A wallet for Starknet that lives on your phone. Hold and send any token, and tip someone who has no wallet at all.

Tip anyone

A tip link works for a person who has never touched a blockchain. They install tip, open the link, and the money is theirs. Nothing is escrowed and nobody holds it on the way.

Self custody, meant literally

  • Keys are made on your phone and stay in its keystore
  • Signing in attaches a name, never a key
  • The recovery phrase is yours and works anywhere

Privacy

tip is built on STRK20, StarkWare’s shielded pool for Starknet. The client for it is written from scratch in Dart and checked against the protocol’s own contracts and the published RFCs.

Shielded transfers are not live yet. They need a proving service, and no public endpoint for one is published. Until that resolves the wallet says a shielded balance is unavailable rather than showing you a zero it cannot vouch for.
