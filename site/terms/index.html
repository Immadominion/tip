What tip is

tip is self-custody wallet software for the Starknet blockchain. It runs on your phone. It is not a bank, an exchange, a broker, or a custodian, and we never hold, control, or have access to your funds at any point.

Your keys are yours, and so is the responsibility

We cannot recover your wallet. Your recovery phrase is generated on your device. If you lose it and lose the device, the funds are gone permanently. Nobody, including us, can restore them, reverse it, or make an exception.

If you choose to make an encrypted backup, the phrase is sealed on your device with a password only you know and we store the sealed result. We cannot open it, and a lawful demand for it would yield unreadable data rather than your phrase. Forgetting that password is the same as losing the phrase: there is no reset, because there is nothing on our side to reset it with.

We cannot reverse a transaction. Once a transfer is accepted by the network it is final. Sending to a wrong address means the funds are gone.

Write your recovery phrase down and keep it somewhere only you can reach. Anyone who reads it controls your money completely.

Fees

Starknet charges a fee for every transaction, paid in STRK to the network rather than to us. A transaction that fails still costs its fee. We do not take a cut of transfers or tips.

Tip links

A tip link holds funds in a throwaway account, and the link itself contains the key to it. This makes it a bearer instrument: whoever reads the link can claim it. Posting one publicly, or sending it over a medium that keeps history, means anyone who can see it can take it. Treat a tip link the way you would treat cash.

Privacy features

tip is built on STRK20, a shielded pool operated as part of the Starknet protocol and not by us. Shielded transfers are available in the app and have been carried out on Starknet's Sepolia test network; they depend on a proving service, and the app tells you when it cannot reach one. Ordinary transfers are fully public, and the app says so on every screen where it matters. No privacy feature is a guarantee of anonymity against a determined and well resourced observer.
