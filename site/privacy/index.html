Privacy

Last updated 24 August 2026

tip is a wallet. Wallets that quietly collect things are the reason this one is written the way it is, so this page is specific rather than broad.

What never leaves your phone

Your recovery phrase, the private key derived from it, your STRK20 viewing key, and your activity history are generated on your device and held in the platform keystore, which is the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore.

We have no copy of any of them and no way to obtain one. Not on request, not under compulsion. There is nothing on our side to hand over. This also means we cannot recover your wallet if you lose the phrase.

What we collect when you sign in

Signing in is optional. The wallet works without it. If you do sign in, we receive and store:

  • your email address, if you use the email code, or
  • your email address, display name or username, and the account identifier your provider issues, if you use Google or X.

It is stored by Supabase, who host authentication for us, and it exists so that your wallet can be carried to a second device, and so a future version can let people tip you by name rather than by sixty-four hexadecimal digits.

Signing in does not give us your keys and does not let us move your money.

If you make an encrypted backup

Backing up is optional and separate from signing in. If you choose to make one, we store one further thing: a sealed blob of data holding your recovery phrase.

It is sealed on your device before it reaches us, with a key stretched from a password only you know. We hold the result and cannot open it. A breach of that table, or a lawful demand for its contents, yields unreadable data and an offline guessing problem rather than anybody’s funds.

Nothing identifying is stored beside it. Not your wallet address, not your public key, not which chain you use. The row is already tied to your account, and adding the address would hand us the exact link between a person and their on-chain history that the rest of this product exists to avoid.

You can delete it at any time, and deleting your account deletes it too. Forgetting the password is the same as losing the phrase: there is no reset, because there is nothing on our side to reset it with.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics and no usage tracking of any kind
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party trackers
  • No location, contacts, photos, or device fingerprinting
  • No record of your balances, transactions, or who you pay
  • No readable copy of your recovery phrase, backed up or otherwise

What the camera does

The camera is used only to read a QR code, and only while that screen is open. Frames are decoded on the device and discarded. No image is stored, and none is transmitted anywhere.

What other people can see regardless

Starknet is a public ledger. An ordinary transfer publishes the sending address, the receiving address, the amount, and the time, permanently and to everyone. That is a property of the blockchain, not a choice we made, and nothing in this policy changes it.

To read balances the app talks to public Starknet nodes, currently operated by Cartridge, Zan and Lava. Those operators can see the IP address your request came from and the address it asked about. We do not control them and they are not bound by this policy. Their own terms apply.

Keeping it or deleting it

The sign-in record is kept until you ask for it to be deleted. Email privacy@usetip.xyz and we will remove the account and everything attached to it.

Removing the wallet from Settings erases the phrase and the activity log from your device. Note that this does not touch anything on the blockchain, which cannot be erased by anyone.

Children

tip is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version is posted here with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be announced in the app.
